SMS Messaging Consent

Straticom AI LLC · Santa Rosa, California · chris@straticom.ai
This page describes exactly how Straticom AI collects consent to send SMS text messages, what messages are sent, and how to stop them. It is provided for customers and for carrier and messaging-provider review.

How consent is collected

Straticom AI collects mobile phone numbers through the consultation booking form on https://www.straticom.ai/#book.

On that form, the visitor enters their mobile number and separately ticks an optional, unchecked checkbox to consent to receive text messages. The checkbox is not pre-selected. Consent is not a condition of booking a consultation — the form can be submitted without it.

The exact consent language on the form

“I agree to receive appointment-related text messages from Straticom AI at the mobile number provided. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy.”

What messages are sent

TypeExample
Appointment confirmation“Straticom AI: Your gap analysis call is confirmed for Tue 3:00pm PT. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.”
Appointment reminder“Straticom AI: Reminder — your consultation is tomorrow at 3:00pm PT. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.”
Scheduling follow-up“Straticom AI: We missed you today. Reply with a time that works and we’ll rebook. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.”

These are conversational, appointment-related messages only. Straticom AI does not send marketing or promotional text messages, and does not send text messages to purchased, rented or third-party lists.

Frequency, rates, opt-out and help

  • Message frequency varies and depends on your scheduled appointments.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out. You will receive one confirmation and no further messages.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or email chris@straticom.ai.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

No mobile information will be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All opt-in data is used solely to deliver the messages you requested. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
