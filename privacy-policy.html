SMS / Text Messaging — Opt-In and Data Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Our booking and messaging providers may process mobile information on our behalf only to provide the service you requested. They may not use it for their own marketing.

How we collect SMS opt-in. Straticom AI collects mobile phone numbers through the Send a Message and Book a Discovery Call forms at https://www.straticom.ai/#book. The visitor enters their own number and separately ticks an optional, unchecked consent checkbox. Consent is not a condition of submitting an inquiry, booking or purchase; the forms submit either way.

What we send. Appointment confirmations, appointment reminders, and scheduling follow-up messages for inquiries or consultations submitted through straticom.ai. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages.

Opt out. Text STOP to any message to opt out at any time. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages. Text HELP for assistance, or email chris@straticom.ai.

Rates and frequency. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Age restriction. You must be 18+ (at least 18 years of age) to consent to receive text messages from Straticom AI.

Carrier liability. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Full SMS terms are set out in our SMS Terms.

1. What information we collect

  • Contact information you give us: name, email address and phone number, entered by you on the inquiry or booking form.
  • Consent records: whether you ticked the SMS consent checkbox and the call-recording consent checkbox, with the date and time.
  • Engagement information: systems in use, workflow notes and spend figures that you choose to share during a discovery call or gap analysis.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type and pages viewed, collected automatically by our website and analytics tools.
  • Call recordings and transcripts, where you have consented to recording.

2. How we use your information

  • To schedule, confirm and remind you about consultations.
  • To send the appointment-related text messages you opted in to receive.
  • To perform the gap analysis and deliver the report you requested.
  • To respond to your questions and provide support.
  • To meet legal, tax and recordkeeping obligations.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that produces legal effects, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. How we share information

We share personal information only with service providers who process it on our behalf under contract — for example our CRM and scheduling platform, our email provider and our messaging carrier — and only to the extent needed to deliver the service you asked for.

Support providers process mobile information only as needed for our booking and messaging services. They do not use it for their own marketing. Our SMS opt-in records are used to honor and document your messaging choice.

If a software vendor needs your business data to scope a pilot, you decide what is shared and when. We broker the introduction; we do not transfer your data on your behalf without your instruction.

4. How we use AI

We use AI to draft, summarize and accelerate the work — not to replace the judgment behind it. Every recommendation that leaves a report is reviewed by a person before it reaches you. We do not put your confidential business data into consumer AI tools. Where AI assists an engagement it runs in enterprise-tier, permissioned environments with logging and data-use controls, and your inputs are not used to train shared foundation models.

5. Cookies and tracking

Our website uses cookies to operate the site and its forms and, with your consent, for other purposes described in the Cookie Policy linked in the website footer. You can manage cookie preferences through the website's privacy controls or your browser settings.

6. Data security

Information is held in access-controlled, password-protected systems with multi-factor authentication, and is transmitted over encrypted (TLS) connections. Access is limited to the person delivering your engagement; we do not hand your data to a junior team or an offshore reviewer. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data retention

We keep booking and consent records for as long as needed to deliver the service and to evidence consent, and then for the period required by law. When an engagement ends you keep the full report; we retain only what we need to support the work delivered.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Unsubscribe from texts: reply STOP to any message.
  • Unsubscribe from email: use the unsubscribe link in any email, or write to us.
  • Access, correct or update the personal information we hold about you.
  • Delete your personal information, including any call recording or transcript.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information — we do not sell or share it, so there is nothing to opt out of, but you may confirm this with us.
  • Non-discrimination: we will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.

To make a privacy request, email chris@straticom.ai or call (707) 395-6520. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

9. Call recording and transcription

Consultation calls may be recorded and transcribed using third-party note-taking software to capture accurate notes and action items. California is an all-party consent state, and we obtain your consent before any recording begins. Recordings and transcripts are used internally and are not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Email chris@straticom.ai to request deletion.

10. Children

Our services are not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
